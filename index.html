Performing the Self Online

Curated by Anjali Naik

Curatorial Statement

This exhibition explores how identity is performed and constructed on the Internet. Rather than simply reflecting reality, online spaces allow individuals to shape, exaggerate, and even fabricate versions of themselves. Works like My Boyfriend Came Back from the War and Excellences & Perfections focus on storytelling and persona-building, showing how identity can be interactive and audience-driven. In contrast, We Feel Fine collects real emotions from across the web, transforming personal expression into shared data. Additionally, Life Sharing pushes this idea further by exposing private digital life to the public. Together, these works highlight how identity online exists between authenticity and performance, shaped by participation, surveillance, and digital structures.

Artworks

My Boyfriend Came Back from the War (1996)

Artist: Olia Lialina

An interactive web narrative that uses frames and hyperlinks to tell a fragmented story about relationships and communication.

Excellences & Perfections (2014)

Artist: Amalia Ulman

A performance-based Instagram project where Ulman created a fictional persona, questioning authenticity and identity on social media.

We Feel Fine (2005)

Artists: Jonathan Harris & Sep Kamvar

A website that collects emotions from blog posts and turns them into a shared, visual experience of human feeling online.

View Work

Life Sharing (2000)

Artists: Eva and Franco Mattes

A project where the artists shared the entire contents of their computer online, blurring the boundary between private and public identity.
